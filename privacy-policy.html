Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nationality Test by Face, currently displayed as VibeCheck in the Android app ("the app"), handles information. It applies to the Android application with package name com.cukkacreatives.skinscanner. "We" and "our" refer to the developer identified in the contact section below.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not require account registration. The app uses:

  • Photos: Images you take or select and submit for AI analysis.
  • Analysis requests: The selected tool, language, country setting, app version, an app-specific device identifier, and premium status sent with your photo. Country settings may be initialized from your network country or device locale.
  • App preferences: Language, country, onboarding progress, and similar settings stored on your device.
  • Analysis history: Analysis status, results, and photo thumbnails stored locally to display your scan history. The server also processes analysis status and results to deliver them to the app.
  • Optional feedback: The message you write, your app version, and an app-specific identifier generated on your device.
  • Advertising and purchase data: Device, usage, ad interaction, and transaction information processed by Google services as described below.
  • Analytics: App interactions such as onboarding steps, tool selection, analysis completion or errors, result sharing, and subscription events (which may include purchase identifiers and diagnostic details), along with device and app information processed by Google Analytics for Firebase.

2. How We Use Your Photos

Photos you submit are sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our AI analysis service to produce the result you request. After processing is complete, the submitted photo is deleted from our server.

3. Local Data Storage

Analysis results, history, app preferences, premium status, and review state may be stored locally on your device. You can remove this information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Removing local data does not itself send a deletion request to our server or cancel a Google Play subscription. Share cards you send to other apps are subject to those apps' storage and privacy practices.

4. Third-Party Services

Our AI analysis service processes submitted photos as described above. The app also uses Google services:

  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements and may process device identifiers, diagnostics, usage data, and ad interactions under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: Handles subscriptions, purchases, and review prompts under Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Helps us understand app usage, feature performance, and errors. It processes app events and related device information as described in Firebase's privacy information.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or trade personal information. Information is shared only with service providers when needed for AI analysis, advertising, analytics, purchases, review prompts, security, and optional feedback handling, or when required by law.

6. Data Retention and Security

Submitted photos are deleted from our server after analysis. Optional feedback and related technical details may be retained as needed to investigate reports, improve the service, and prevent abuse. We use reasonable technical measures to protect information in transit and during processing, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Deletion Requests

The app has no user accounts. You can delete locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Submitted analysis photos are automatically deleted from our server after processing.

To request deletion of optional feedback or other information that may be associated with your app-specific identifier, email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject “Nationality Test by Face Data Deletion Request”. Include relevant scan IDs or feedback details, if available, so we can locate the information. Do not send another face photo or identity document.

8. Your Choices

You can choose not to submit a photo, decline optional feedback, manage advertising choices through your device or Google settings, and manage or cancel subscriptions through your Google Play account. Camera access is used to take a photo when you choose that option; you can also choose an existing image through Android's image selector. Notification permissions can be managed in Android settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Medical and Entertainment Notice

The app is not a medical application. Its photo tools provide speculative, AI-generated impressions for entertainment and may be inaccurate. A photo cannot establish nationality, ethnicity, ancestry, identity, personality, or objective attractiveness. Age estimates do not verify actual age. Results do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

11. International Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Privacy and data-protection laws in those countries may differ from those where you live.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with a new update date.
